Jaruri is a free and open-source on-device tracker for money and time.

Features:
• Feature hub to open Money or Time
• Money: dashboard with month filter, search, income/expense/balance
• Manual transactions plus Indian bank/UPI/credit-card SMS import
• Accounts (cash, bank, credit cards) and monthly category budgets
• Charts, categories, and CSV backup/restore
• Time: manual time log with monthly history (Digital Wellbeing does not export usable history)
• Optional demo history for trying the money UI
• No Google Play Services, no ads, no tracking, no required account

SMS reading is opt-in (manual import or Settings auto-import). Your data never leaves the device unless you export a CSV.
